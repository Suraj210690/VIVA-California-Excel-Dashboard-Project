I’m excited to share my latest Excel Dashboard project, built to analyze sales, customer behavior, product performance, delivery, and satisfaction.

I used the STAR method to approach this project:

⭐ Situation

Businesses often have large amounts of sales and customer data, but raw data alone doesn’t provide a clear picture of performance.

The challenge was to bring different business metrics together into one interactive and easy-to-understand dashboard.

🎯 Task

The objective was to create an Excel dashboard that could help answer key business questions:
How are sales performing over time?
Which products are most popular?
Which ordering channels are customers using?
How many products do customers typically purchase?
Where are customers located?
How long does it take to ship orders?
How satisfied are customers?

🛠️ Action

I transformed the raw data into an interactive dashboard using Microsoft Excel, applying:

🔹 Data cleaning & preparation
 🔹 Pivot Tables
 🔹 Pivot Charts
 🔹 Slicers & filters
 🔹 KPI cards
 🔹 Trend analysis
 🔹 Customer segmentation
 🔹 Product performance analysis
 🔹 Geographic analysis
 🔹 Dashboard design & visualization

The dashboard brings key metrics together, including 2,400 orders, 11,997 quantity sold, $649K amount, 4.0 average rating, and 2.3 days average delivery time.

📈 Result

The final dashboard provides a single view of business performance, making it easier to identify trends, understand customer behavior, evaluate product performance, and monitor operational metrics.

💡 My biggest takeaway:

 A good dashboard isn't just about making data look attractive. It's about converting data into a story that helps people understand the business and make better decisions.
